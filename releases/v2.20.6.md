# Manager Board v2.20.6 — Evidence-Based Promotion Justifications

This release improves Employee Plans recommendations into formal, evidence-based leadership justifications.

- Develops specific observed work and manager feedback into paragraphs explaining the contribution and its significance for the proposed advancement.
- Uses separate local drafting stages with visible progress, cancellation and bounded retries. A failed or cancelled generation keeps the existing preview.
- Uses recorded matching-role readiness to support a direct proposed decision, while preserving explicit conditions, outstanding required path items and unassessed-month distinctions.
- Adds first-line paragraph indentation in formatted previews, saved views and exports.
- Uses compact letter formatting with one title. Without Evidence exports keep leadership text clean; With Evidence exports retain citations and start supporting records separately.

Manager review remains required. The generator can remove unsupported statements, but its checks do not establish the accuracy of every claim. Drafting never saves or approves a recommendation automatically, and a proposed promotion is not a granted HR decision.

Schema 52 is unchanged. Existing plans, saved reviews, recommendation snapshots and Workspace/Vault portability are preserved. Local AI remains optional and uses a separately supplied model; no model or employee data is bundled.
