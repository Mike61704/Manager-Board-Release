# Manager Board v2.19.1 — Custom Career Template Assignment

- **Choose approved custom requirements:** when a career goal has no exact role match, select a saved approved template from the dropdown. Matching paths can also choose different requirements.
- **Review role differences deliberately:** see the template's role scope, explain why its requirements fit the employee's goal, and confirm manager review. The explanation stays in manager-only assignment activity. Draft and retired templates remain unavailable.
- **Assign from Settings:** approved templates have **Assign to employee** under Settings > People & Aliases > Path to Promotion Templates and in template review. Choose an active employee to open the same career-plan wizard. An existing plan opens for review rather than being replaced.
- **Keep unfinished work:** dates and private manager notes survive switching requirements. Template choice and role-review confirmation recover through the normal draft workflow; changing the roles clears the confirmation.

Schema 49 is unchanged. Employee profile titles, approved template versions, existing plans, progress and immutable completed 1:1 references remain preserved. Employee-facing views and exports exclude private review explanations. Templates and manager review do not establish HR promotion approval or automatically credit previous training. Optional Local AI models remain separate files.

After updating, open Employee Plans > Career Development / Promotion Requirements. Set the goal, choose the approved requirements and review before saving. You can also start with Assign to employee from an approved template in Settings.
