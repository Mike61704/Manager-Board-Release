# Manager Board v2.19.0 — Employee Career Development

- **Five-card Employee Plans center:** Career Development / Promotion Requirements and Progress Tracking join Development Plan, Employee Expectations and Manager Review.
- **Start with the employee's goal:** choose the current and target roles, review matching approved requirements, and create a career plan. Current-role training is supported too.
- **Separate template management:** manage Path to Promotion Templates under Settings > People & Aliases beside Onboarding templates. Customize an existing template into a new draft version, save changes, review, explicitly approve, or delete/retire a template.
- **More useful starting suggestions:** fourteen editable draft templates cover Cloud and Platform role progressions and general training, with 160 items describing outcomes, evidence and manager review points. Choose which suggestions fit your organization; they are not approved HR criteria, mandatory certifications, or automatic promotion decisions.
- **Evidence-based progress:** record completion, evidence, dates and manager verification. Employee-safe views and exports exclude manager-private notes. Confirmed reset and deletion controls preserve the relevant audit and immutable historical meeting references.
- **Durable, deliberate history:** unfinished edits recover through the existing draft workflow. Assigned template versions remain frozen; later template customization never rewrites existing plans. Historical 1:1 references are explicit snapshots.

This release upgrades the Workspace to schema 49 with a portable backup before migration. Keep that backup when upgrading; installing an older application does not reverse the schema. Existing Development Plan, Employee Expectations, Manager Review, 1:1 records, Workfront behavior and Workspace/Vault boundaries are preserved. Local AI models remain optional separate files.

After updating, open People > employee > Employee Plans. Prepare and approve your organization's requirements in Settings > People & Aliases > Path to Promotion Templates, then create a plan and track evidence. Bundled suggestions are saved as drafts for manager review and do not automatically replace your templates or assign employee plans.
