# Manager Board v2.16.0 — Employee Onboarding

- **Onboarding on the employee card:** a compact strip below Current Focus and Responsibilities opens an independent employee checklist and shows saved completion progress.
- **Reusable templates:** begin with a general starter or create your team's own phases, items, owners and due-day offsets. Manage templates from the checklist or Settings > People & Aliases > Onboarding Templates. Editing a template preserves existing employee checklists.
- **Saved progress:** track owners, due dates, notes, completion timestamps and not-applicable items. Progress and templates survive restarts, normal backups, restore and Vault publication. Unfinished-draft recovery and revision checks protect edits.
- **Room to work:** wider responsive editors and confirmed deletion let you manage checklists and custom templates. Deleting a template preserves started employee checklists. Add checklist item and export controls stay together below the last item.
- **Clear team exports:** HTML and PDF include green completed checks, empty unfinished boxes, written statuses, phase totals, readable dates and a progress summary. XLSX includes completion markers, status colors, filters and formatted dates. CSV retains structured values for reuse.

After updating, open People and an employee, then Start onboarding. Choose a template and start date, save progress and export the saved checklist in HTML, PDF, XLSX or CSV. Team-specific items can be maintained in custom template copies.

Existing projects, Workfront behavior, saved sorting and column widths, backups, themes, licenses and Workspace/Vault continuity are preserved. Schema 48 is unchanged. Local AI models remain optional separate files.
