# Manager Board v2.20.4 — Employee Plans History and Recommendations

This release improves Employee Plans and makes leadership recommendations easier to prepare and share.

- Reopens archived, superseded and ended-early development plans with their original manager reviews, ratings, notes and selected evidence.
- Organizes Employee Plans into Development Plans, Path to Promotion and Recommendations, with historical plans and reviews below them.
- Adds role dropdowns and an editable quick summary, followed by evidence-grounded Local AI recommendation drafting with visible progress and cancellation.
- Keeps recommendation previews editable, saving separate from named manager approval, and promotion decisions under human control.
- Offers HTML and PDF exports without evidence for a clean leadership narrative, or with evidence and bracketed references for supporting detail.

Early closure and unassessed months are distinct from performance failures. Existing review history and evidence are preserved. Schema 52 adds closure metadata and saved recommendation versions; the normal pre-migration safety backup remains in place. Existing monthly review and intake fixes from v2.20.3 are retained.

Local AI remains optional and works in process with a separately supplied model. No model or employee data is bundled. Review generated wording and evidence before approving a recommendation; AI output is not an HR promotion approval.
