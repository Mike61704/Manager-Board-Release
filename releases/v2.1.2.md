# Manager Board v2.1.2 — Clearer Diagnostics

Manager Board’s Diagnostics & Logs center is now easier to scan, navigate, and
reset after reviewed issues, while retaining the evidence needed for support.

## What’s new

- Consolidates active findings into one focused review area and groups session
  history, technical details, full logs, retention, and cleanup behind a single
  secondary section.
- Keeps error findings, the System health checks section, and individual health
  checks collapsed until you choose to inspect them.
- Preserves complete plain-language explanations, possible effects, recommended
  actions, technical evidence, verification, and support export controls.
- Adds **Clear findings** to acknowledge reviewed errors and warnings without
  deleting their underlying operational history.
- Stops an expected optional development-file warning from appearing in public
  installations, labels repeated findings from earlier sessions as historical,
  and uses the computer’s local calendar date in diagnostic export filenames.

## Privacy and continuity

- Clearing findings preserves operational logs and support-export evidence.
- Live Workspace, Vault, backup, runtime, and Local AI checks remain truthful and
  cannot be cleared while their condition still needs attention.
- Diagnostic logs remain content-limited and exclude employee records, notes,
  attachments, AI prompts, and chat responses.
- Offline activation, secure updates, Workfront safety, Local AI isolation,
  backups, restore, the First-Run Welcome Guide, session privacy, and every
  Workspace/Vault boundary remain intact.
- The complete CPU, Vulkan, OpenVINO Intel NPU, and TBB runtime remains included.
- No source code, private signing material, organization data, user data, or
  GGUF model is included.
