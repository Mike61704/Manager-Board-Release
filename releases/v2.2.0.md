# Manager Board v2.2.0 — Manager 1:1 Center

Manager Board now includes a private, upward-facing 1:1 workspace for preparing
the work, issues, grievances, decisions, and support requests you need to discuss
with your own manager or director. This is preparation for the conversation, not
a review or rating of your manager.

## What’s new

- Adds **My 1:1 Center** at the top of Manager Center, with next-meeting details,
  preparation, commitments, completed history, and individual/history exports.
- Lets Manager Work, employee 1:1 records, and standard Notes be explicitly
  flagged for the upward agenda while keeping each source type clearly labeled.
- Keeps unresolved flagged source items available for later meetings until they
  are unflagged or archived; saved meeting selections become immutable snapshots.
- Defaults the agenda to the period since the previous manager 1:1 and provides
  14-, 30-, 60-, 90-day, and All flagged lookback choices.
- Adds source-labeled recommendations, rich-text preparation fields, and private
  Local AI Assist for progress, workload, team issues, blockers, decisions,
  support requests, discussion notes, and commitments.
- Adds theme-aware HTML exports for individual meetings and complete 1:1 history.
- Adds optional **Reports to** name and title fields to Manager Profile and the
  first-run Welcome Guide so My 1:1 Center can populate the manager/director
  context automatically while preserving explicit meeting-specific overrides.

## Privacy and continuity

- Your manager or director remains outside People and Leaderboard records.
- Manager-private preparation remains separate from employee-facing shared 1:1
  content, and employee 1:1 notes enter the upward agenda only when explicitly
  flagged.
- Schema v44 preserves existing boards through normal migration and keeps every
  accepted Workspace, Vault, backup, restore, Workfront, activation, update,
  Help, Welcome Guide, Diagnostics, and Local AI boundary intact.
- The complete CPU, Vulkan, OpenVINO Intel NPU, and TBB runtime remains included.
- No source code, private signing material, organization data, user data, or
  GGUF model is included.
