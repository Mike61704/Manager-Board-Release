# Manager Board v2.20.2 — Review and Intake Repairs

This maintenance release repairs monthly review evidence, AI-assisted monthly summaries and the Workfront Team Intake template.

- **Current review dates:** A fresh Manager Review reads through today, capped at the saved plan end. Completed months show their full evidence ranges, the current month remains partial and future months are clearly Not Yet Due. Deliberate historical dates and resumed drafts retain their chosen cutoff.
- **More reliable monthly AI summaries:** Draft from Selected Evidence handles supported response formats with exact evidence checks and one bounded repair. Technical work can be explained in coherent prose while preserving completion or pending status and recorded results. Suggestions still require manager review; unsupported output preserves existing notes.
- **Repaired intake workbook:** Export Intake Template produces an Excel workbook with corrected worksheet XML declarations. Project, Tasks and Instructions retain their content, formatting, formulas and dropdowns.

Saved notes, ratings, evidence choices, review history, completed 1:1 records and Workspace/Vault portability are preserved. The data format remains schema 51. Local AI remains optional, local and dependent on a separately supplied model; no model is included.

Download **ManagerBoard-Setup-2.20.2.exe** from this release and install it over your existing Manager Board installation. User data remains separate from replaceable application files.
